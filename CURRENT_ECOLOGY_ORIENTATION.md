# Current Ecology Orientation

**Local class:** INDEPENDENT-LIFECYCLE SURFACE / PUBLIC ARTICLES + HUMAN-FACING PUBLICATION

Radical Love Revolution remains live outside the eight primary roots because publication-ready articles, essays, explainers, social adaptations, and public documents have a distinct editorial/publication lifecycle.

Its independence is a publication function, not a claim of separate project truth authority.

## Current relation

- Source research, evidence, theory, governance, scientific, environmental, relational, and other bodies remain owned by their current living sources.
- RLR translates source-backed work into material that can stand on its own for ordinary readers.
- `UNUM-Lattice-News` remains the evidence-traced public-reality/news surface; broader human-facing derivatives may be published here without duplicating Lattice operations.
- `UNUM-Visitor` may route readers here as one public exploration/publication destination.
- `UNUM-Archives` may preserve meaningful frozen publication states.

Older README pointers to now-retired repositories are historical routes unless current orientation confirms a surviving wall.

## Working route

```text
current source-backed body
-> preserve claim / evidence / uncertainty / provenance
-> translate for public reading
-> publish here in the appropriate form
-> preserve return path to source
-> revise visibly when the source state changes
```

This repository exists to bring useful work to people without making them tour the private internal ecology.

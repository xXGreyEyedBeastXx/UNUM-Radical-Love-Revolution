# UNUM Radical Love Revolution

**Status:** ACTIVE PUBLICATION / PUBLIC-FACING ARTICLE + DOCUMENT SURFACE

`UNUM-Radical-Love-Revolution` is the public-facing publication surface for articles, essays, explainers, posts, and other documents intended for ordinary readers outside the private/internal UNUM repository ecology.

Typical destinations may include:

```text
Facebook
Instagram
Substack
other public article / social / publication surfaces
```

The purpose is not to expose the whole machinery behind an article. It is to translate useful work from across the wider ecology into material that can stand on its own for a public reader.

## What belongs here

- public-facing articles and essays;
- shorter social/publication versions of longer work;
- human-readable explainers derived from research, evidence, theory, governance, environmental, relational, scientific, or other project work;
- publication-ready documents and related public-facing revisions;
- lightly exposed source/provenance pointers where useful for credibility and re-entry;
- public article sections organized by source ecology when that improves navigation.

A public article may be informed by deep internal work without requiring the internal architecture, recovery machinery, tools, roleplay, source trees, or private developmental context to travel with it.

## Authority boundary

This repository is a **publication surface**, not a new research, intake, indexing, portfolio, or project-authority repository.

```text
publication != source authority
public explanation != internal ontology
article != research intake
summary != evidence promotion
readability != permission to erase provenance
```

The owning source repository keeps authority over the underlying research, evidence, theory, governance model, technical mechanism, or other domain claim.

## Primary crossings

```text
specialist / research / evidence repository
-> source-backed public translation
-> Radical Love Revolution article / document
-> public platform
-> optional frozen publication return to Archives
```

Important neighbors:

- `UNUM-Lattice-News` — evidence-traced public-world/news ecology. Human-facing articles derived from that ecology may land here under a Lattice News section/folder without recreating its intake or evidence machinery.
- `UNUM-Lattice-News-Intake` — owns news-specific intake/review before durable claims are earned; do not duplicate that system here.
- `UNUM-Research` — owns general cross-repository research intake, routing, and return watch; Radical Love does not become another suggestions/intake queue.
- `UNUM-Archives` — frozen publication-history / cold return shell for published or superseded article states when preservation is useful.
- `UNUM-Work` — portfolio/capability demonstration. It may point to selected public work, but Radical Love is not primarily a résumé or portfolio surface.
- `Legendary-Disco` — public toys, demos, and interactive play rather than article/document publication.
- `UNUM-Hub` — repository alignment and drift only; it may track this lane but does not author publication content by default.

Other repositories may contribute public-facing material whenever their work produces something genuinely useful to ordinary people. Contribution does not transfer their local authority here.

## Publication posture

When converting internal work into a public article:

```text
identify the actual source seat
-> preserve claim/evidence class
-> decide what a public reader needs
-> translate without laundering uncertainty or harm
-> retain enough provenance to find the source again
-> publish in the appropriate public form
-> preserve a frozen return copy when useful
```

Public-facing does not mean apologetic, flattened, sensationalized, or stripped of causal structure. It means legible outside the private project ecology.

## Organization

Grow folders because publication work actually needs them, not to mirror the internal taxonomy of every source repository.

For example:

```text
Lattice-News/     public articles derived from the Lattice/news ecology
articles/         general longer-form public pieces when useful
social/           platform-sized adaptations when useful
```

These are publication shelves, not duplicate research departments.

## Tiny lock

> Do the deep work where it belongs. Bring the useful part to people. Keep the source. Keep the truth. Do not make the public reader tour the whole house.
